CONCEPT

Narrative meaning:

“Organizations manufacture consensus through vast numbers of synthetic
identities acting in coordination.”

The illustration must communicate:

1. Many synthetic identities initially remain individually visible.
2. Their posture, spacing or rhythm becomes progressively synchronized.
3. That synchronization resolves into one broad, uniform collective
   output.
4. The final output reads as manufactured agreement rather than organic
   human connection.

The image should have a clear left-to-right editorial progression:

distinct repeated synthetic figures
→ increasing alignment
→ one restrained shared output band, waveform or directional field

The figures must remain recognizable as simplified human identities.

TARGETED CORRECTIONS TO CONSENSUS-A

Correct these specific problems:

- Remove the perspective-recession or tunnel-like reading.
- Remove overly busy internal body patterns.
- Avoid figures shrinking toward a vanishing point.
- Establish a broad lateral composition instead.
- Preserve full or mostly full figures.
- Increase negative space.
- Make the final shared output visually simple and unmistakable.
- Keep the composition related to echoes-a through repeated human forms,
  outline style and palette.
