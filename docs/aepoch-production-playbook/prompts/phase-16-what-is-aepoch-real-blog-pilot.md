The blog's research has already been done. Treat the blog as authoritative for
its thesis, claims, narrative intent, terminology, examples, uncertainty, and
exclusions. Research may verify and enrich; it may not silently rewrite,
contradict, dilute, or replace the source. Preserve protected narrative fields
through extraction, research, proposal, and script. If verification finds a
material conflict, stop and present it to the author instead of resolving it
unilaterally.

## Quality gates

The final candidate must demonstrate, at minimum:

- faithful source adaptation with no unsupported invention;
- immediate narrative clarity and a strong opening promise;
- deliberate pacing and meaningful visual change rather than decorated slides;
- bespoke, brand-specific art direction with no generic AI/crypto/cyberpunk look;
- polished motion, transitions, typography, hierarchy, and normal-size legibility;
- clean narration, music, sound design, mix, captions, and word-level sync;
- enough visual and structural variety to sustain the approved runtime;
- deterministic technical output, provenance, cost record, and clean review artifacts;
- explicit side-by-side assessment against the grounded benchmark rubric;
- author approval for public readiness.

## Stop conditions

Stop and report if source verification materially conflicts with the blog, a
required provider/runtime is unavailable, the benchmark videos cannot be
analyzed, cost would exceed the approved amount, or a creative review fails.
Do not silently substitute providers, models, runtimes, authoring modes, audio,
or visual strategy.
