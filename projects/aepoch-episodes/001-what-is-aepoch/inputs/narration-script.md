## Editorial notes

Every substantive edit made to the source outline (`source/planned-narration-outline.md`), in order. Net word count is unchanged (719 → 719); no protected passage (the welcome, the August 9 invitation, "things will break," or "magnificent biological transformer") was touched. No tokenomics, Rootstock, governance, or technical detail was added anywhere.

- **E1 (N03, clarity/rhythm):** "so that you'll have the basic understanding" → "so you'll have the basic understanding." Dropped the redundant "that" for spoken rhythm; no meaning change.
- **E2 (N07, clarity):** "Videos that look like the people but their lips aren't quite synching" → "Videos where the face looks right, but the lips don't quite sync." The original phrasing was grammatically ambiguous ("look like the people" doesn't parse cleanly as a subject); rewritten to say the same thing clearly.
- **E3 (N07, grammar/caption readability):** "any of this sound familiar?" → "Does any of this sound familiar?" Subject-verb agreement fix; also reads better as a standalone caption line.
- **E4 (N10, factual precision + rhythm):** Three short, choppy sentences ("Some people call this the dead internet theory. What used to sound like conspiracy is now backed by data. A recent study found... Projections say that number climbs past 90%...") merged into one flowing sentence with a colon before the statistic. Also changed "climbs past 90%" to "could climb past 90%" — a projection is an estimate, not a certainty, and the softer verb keeps the claim honest without weakening it.
- **E5 (N11, redundancy):** "can't quite explain it - because it's really difficult to know" → "can't quite explain it — it's genuinely hard to know for certain." The original's final clause restated "can't quite explain it" without adding new meaning; the edit specifies *what's* hard to know (whether something is real) instead of just repeating the difficulty.
- **E6 (N12, rhythm/parallelism):** "It extracts data without our knowledge" → "It extracts our data without our knowledge." Small addition of "our" to match the parallel triad that follows ("our time and energy — our presence").
- **E7 (N13, redundancy):** "That system has morphed into something..." → "It's morphed into something..." Avoids repeating "system" twice in adjacent sentences; no meaning change.
- **E8 (N14, grammar/caption readability):** "And it doesn't recognize the one thing that stays uniquely human. Our presence - expressed as attention, care and creativity." (a sentence fragment) → "And it doesn't recognize the one thing that stays uniquely human: our presence, expressed as attention, care and creativity." Joined into one grammatically complete sentence via a colon; wording unchanged.
- **E9 (N25, typo):** Fixed a stray double space ("to  appreciate" → "to appreciate"). No content change.

Structural note: several long paragraphs in the source outline were split across two narration beats (N06/N07, N08/N09, N13/N14, N20/N21, N23/N24) purely for scene-timing and caption-pacing purposes — no words were added, removed, or reordered across these splits.
